Serif headlines, generous reading space, no keyword badges or decorative process cards in these three articles.

Content plan: rewrite the first three existing photo articles independently, preserving their URLs. Gowanus: blue vertical tile, niche placement, and scope boundaries in an older bathroom. Interaction thesis: readers can jump to the decision they need, compare the article's two scope choices, and prepare a neighborhood-specific estimate email with their building type, scope, timing, and callback details. Release decision: publish the evaluated trio together, then prepare and evaluate subsequent groups of three. Recommended pace is one batch per week, subject to actual production and evaluation; this file does not schedule recurring publication. Do not hold the next useful batch until Google indexes the previous one. Maintain canonical URLs and truthful last-modified dates; measure inquiries and estimates separately from clicks and indexing.
